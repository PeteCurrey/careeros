# Career OS — Product Claims Register

> **Core Governance Rule:**
> Product marketing may describe approved future direction confidently, but must never convert future architecture into a statement that a capability is currently deployed.

## Claim Status Taxonomy

* `VERIFIED_CURRENT`: Definite present tense used only where the feature is deployed, tested, and verifiable in production.
* `ILLUSTRATIVE`: Mock interfaces, example recommendations, simulated data, and demo scenarios. Must be clearly but discreetly labeled.
* `DESIGNED_DIRECTION`: Architecture and intended behavior approved as product direction but not yet fully deployed. (Phrasing: *designed to*, *built around*, *intended to*, *the product model uses*).
* `PLANNED`: Explicitly later-stage functionality or future product expansions.
* `PROHIBITED_UNSUPPORTED`: Statements that cannot currently be substantiated, manufactured precision, fake engine versions, or blanket competitive assertions. Strictly prohibited in copy.
